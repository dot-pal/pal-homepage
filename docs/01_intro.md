The Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) was a non-profit community initiative whose mission was to promote and advance open-source security in the Polkadot ecosystem, and to make Polkadot a safer and more attractive ecosystem for builders and users.

:::warning
PAL has concluded — no further funding requests are being processed. Existing engagements continue per their agreements. The pages below are retained for historical reference.
:::

Through [funding](/funding) from the Polkadot Treasury, PAL supported a range of security initiatives:

* Audits of [parachains](02_funding/01_parachains.md) and [smart contracts](02_funding/02_smart_contracts.md) secured by Polkadot.
* Audits of [common-good functionality](02_funding/03_common_good.md).
* Development of open-source [Security Tooling](02_funding/04_security_tooling.md) made available to the Polkadot ecosystem.
* Other security initiatives aligned with PAL's mission.

PAL also collaborated with industry-leading security experts such as the [Security Alliance](https://securityalliance.org/) to bring their initiatives into Polkadot.

To learn more about the activities of PAL you can check out our [community reports](/community_reports).
