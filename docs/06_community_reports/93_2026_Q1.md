# PAL Community Report 2026 / Q1
Date: 26.05.2026

This Community report summarizes the activity of the Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) during the period January-March 2026.

## PAL

The Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) is a community-driven initiative that aims to make Polkadot a safer and more attractive place for both builders and users by allocating funds from the Polkadot Treasury ([bounty #22](https://polkadot.subscan.io/bounty/22)) to advance security in Rust / Polkadot SDK.

PAL provides funding for audits (Rust and Solidity), security tooling, bug bounties, and common-good security initiatives. Find out more about the various funding opportunities on [PAL’s homepage](https://dotpal.io).

## Summary
In the period of January through March 2026, PAL provided funding for **1 large audit**, co-funded **1 bug bounty payout**, and disbursed **Milestone 2** of the ecosystem monitoring tool, making Q1 2026 the highest-spend quarter on record for PAL at **133,692 DOT** in total.

The headline of Q1 was the co-funding of an in-depth audit of [Moonbeam](https://moonbeam.network/) by [SRLabs](https://www.srlabs.de/) for **51,309 DOT**, PAL’s single largest audit contribution to date. The engagement covered roughly 180,000 LOC across Moonbeam runtimes 4200, 4300 and 4400, and identified **1 high** and **1 low** severity issue, both since mitigated. The full report is now [available](/audit_reports/26q1/moonbeam-srlabs-2601.pdf).

On the tooling side, Ocelloids delivered **Milestone 2** of [RFP 2501 - the ecosystem monitoring tool](/rfp/2501), with **37,251 DOT** paid out.

PAL also co-funded a bug bounty payout on [Acala](https://acala.network/) for **23,476 DOT**, continuing its support for ecosystem-wide responsible disclosure programs.

Finally, the curator team composition changed during the quarter: **Pierre stepped down as a PAL curator** at the end of February 2026, and the team operated with **5 curators** during March 2026.

### Other
The PAL curators have received in total 21,656 DOT as remuneration for their work. In January 2026 this corresponded to $3,000 per curator, while starting in February 2026 the curator salary was lowered to $1,500 per curator per month, using the 7d EMA DOT price.
