# PAL Community Report 2026 / Q2
Date: 16.06.2026

This Community report summarizes the activity of the Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) during the period April-June 2026. The quarter is still in progress; figures reflect activity through mid-June 2026 and may be updated in the final version of this report.

## PAL

The Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) was a community-driven initiative that aimed to make Polkadot a safer and more attractive place for both builders and users by allocating funds from the Polkadot Treasury ([bounty #22](https://polkadot.subscan.io/bounty/22)) to advance security in Rust / Polkadot SDK.

Over its lifetime, PAL funded audits (Rust and Solidity), security tooling, bug bounties, and common-good security initiatives. PAL has now concluded — no further funding requests are being processed; existing engagements continue per their agreements. Find out more on [PAL's homepage](https://dotpal.io).

## Summary
In the period of April through June 2026, PAL focused its activity on the **Ocelloids ecosystem monitoring tool**, with no new audits or bug bounty payouts processed during the quarter.

The headline of Q2 was the delivery of **Milestone 3** of [RFP 2501 - the ecosystem monitoring tool](/rfp/2501) by [Ocelloids](https://www.ocelloids.net/), which expanded the platform's DeFi monitoring capabilities to [Hydration](https://hydration.net/), [Moonbeam](https://moonbeam.network/) and [Bifrost](https://bifrost.io/), introduced alerting templates for money market health and DEX liquidity pool TVL, and shipped TVL, volume, money market, and liquid staking analytics in the Console UI. **Milestone 4** was also paid during the quarter; its delivery report is still pending and will be added to this report once available. In the same payout, PAL also covered **12 months of infrastructure, operations, and maintenance** for the platform, as foreseen in the RFP.
