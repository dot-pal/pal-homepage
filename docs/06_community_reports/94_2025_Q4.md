# PAL Community Report 2025 / Q4
Date: 26.05.2026

This Community report summarizes the activity of the Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) during the period October-December 2025.

## PAL

The Polkadot Assurance Legion (PAL) is a community-driven initiative that aims to make Polkadot a safer and more attractive place for both builders and users by allocating funds from the Polkadot Treasury ([bounty #22](https://polkadot.subscan.io/bounty/22)) to advance security in Rust / Polkadot SDK.

PAL provides funding for audits (Rust and Solidity), security tooling, bug bounties, and common-good security initiatives. Find out more about the various funding opportunities on [PAL’s homepage](https://dotpal.io).

## Summary
In the period of October through December 2025, PAL provided funding for **3 audits** covering **2 different teams**, co-funded **2 bug bounty payouts**, and supported the first delivery milestone of the ecosystem monitoring tool.

The highlight of Q4 was the kick-off and first milestone delivery of [RFP 2501 - the ecosystem monitoring tool](/rfp/2501) built by [Ocelloids](https://www.ocelloids.net/), the vendor selected at the end of Q3. Milestone 1 was paid out in December 2025, marking the official start of execution on PAL’s largest tooling initiative to date.

On the audit side, PAL co-funded a comprehensive review of [Acurast](https://acurast.com/) by Monethic (split across two engagements), which uncovered **1 critical**, **5 high**, **9 medium** and **9 low** severity issues across more than 10,500 LOC, the highest number of high-severity findings in a single quarter. PAL also co-funded a focused audit of Bifrost by Beosin (362 LOC, 1 medium-severity finding).

Finally, Q4 saw PAL co-fund two bug bounty payouts on [Hydration](https://hydration.net/), continuing its support for ecosystem-wide responsible disclosure.
